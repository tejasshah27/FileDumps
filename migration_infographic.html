Why the hybrid approach wins

SPEED vs. RELIABILITY — three approaches, mapped
Speed
Routine changes land across the whole portfolio in a fraction of the time a fully manual or fully AI-driven pass would take.
Cost
Engineering and compute effort goes only where judgment is genuinely needed — not spent re-doing work a tool already does reliably.
Quality & Risk
Consistent, auditable changes cut rework and defects compared with fully AI-generated migrations at this scale.
